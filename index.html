What it reads

A context graph. Every message, call and meeting, resolved onto the people and companies they belong to.

Across your stack

Gmail cannot join a call transcript to a job change to a calendar invite. Each search stands alone.

One graph, not many searches. The email, the transcript and the invite land on the same record, so one answer can cross all three.

When a fact changes

It returns what it finds, including the line that stopped being true eighteen months ago. Search has no idea.

Auditable. The old fact is struck, dated and kept. You get what is true now, and can still ask what your firm believed in 2024.

A paraphrase of some documents, as good as whatever happened to be retrieved that time.

Deterministic. The same answer every time you ask, with the document behind it one click away.
