What firms ask about the money.

Time. An engineer connects each system, resolves duplicate contacts, and reads your history back. Firms that do this themselves spend months. We do it in 14 days.

fein keeps running. It is on your servers, the licence does not expire, and the graph is in your database. We cannot switch it off.
