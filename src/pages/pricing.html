What firms ask about the money.

If the software is free, what am I paying for?

Time. An engineer connects each system, resolves duplicate contacts, and reads your history back. Firms that do this themselves spend months. We do it in 14 days.

You do not buy access. The binary is the same one on GitHub, and nothing is gated.

What is the difference between the plans?

How much we keep doing after the setup. Core keeps what we built running. Plus adds new answers when your team asks new questions. Pro adds new connectors when you adopt new tools, and a named engineer who knows your stack.

The software is identical on every plan. You can move between them at any time.

What happens if we stop paying?

fein keeps running. It is on your servers, the licence does not expire, and the graph is in your database. We cannot switch it off.

The upkeep stops. When a connector breaks, the fix becomes your job.

Is it per seat?

No. The plans differ by how much we keep doing, never by seat count. Every person at the firm can query the graph, and so can every agent they run. Adding people never changes the price.

We are a small fund. Is the build fee negotiable?

No. The work is the same for every firm. If the fee is out of reach, run it yourself for free. It is the same software.
