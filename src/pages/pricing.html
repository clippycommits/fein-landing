What firms ask about the money.

If the software is free, what am I paying for?

Time. An engineer connects each system, resolves duplicate contacts, and reads your history back. Firms that do this themselves spend months; we do it in 14 days. The binary you get is the same one on GitHub.

What is the difference between the plans?

Who does the work. Self-hosted is the same software, run and maintained by your engineers. Managed is the same software, built into your stack and kept running by ours. There is no third plan and no feature gate between them.

What happens if we stop paying?

fein keeps running. It is on your servers, the licence does not expire, and the graph stays in your database. Only the upkeep stops: the next broken connector is yours to fix.

Is it per seat?

No. Every person at the firm, and every agent they run, can query the graph at one price.

We are a small fund. Is the price negotiable?

No, and it does not need to be: $450 is already the same figure for every firm, so a four-person fund pays what a forty-person one pays. If it is still out of reach, run it yourself for free: it is the same software, and we will point you at the docs.
