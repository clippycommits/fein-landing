What platform teams ask first.

We already track the portfolio in Notion and Airtable. Why do we need this?

A tracker holds what somebody typed into it after the meeting. It does not know that a promise made at the June board is still open, or that the candidate a founder needs emailed a partner last week. fein reads the record your firm already makes, files it to the company it belongs to, and keeps it current with nobody maintaining it.

The tracker can stay. fein reads it too.

Where does our data go?

Your data goes nowhere. fein runs on your infrastructure and sends nothing to us. There is no telemetry, and no control plane of ours in the path. Every line of code that touches your data is public. You can read it before you run anything.

What access does your engineer have?

The engineer works inside your environment, on access your IT grants. You can revoke it at any time. Our people are bound by confidentiality, and access is limited to those who need it to support the service. Nothing is granted permanently. Access ends when the build ends, unless you ask us to keep the connectors supported.

One thing we will not overstate: fein does not today keep a tamper-proof audit trail of administrative access. Your own logging is the record that counts. The graph also sits on your machine unencrypted above the volume, so whoever you give host access to can read it. That is true of your other systems too, and it is the reason the machine is yours and not ours.

Can board material stay restricted to the partner who holds the seat?

Access is per person. Each person’s sensitive sources live in a private layer inside the shared graph, and fein enforces that scoping inside every query. A board pack can stay with the partner who holds the seat while the promise ledger and the support record stay shared. The detail is on the security page.

How far back does it read?

fein reads as far back as your mail retention allows. Most firms give us years of history on the first pass, which usually covers each company from the first meeting on. On day fourteen fein answers like a colleague who sat through every board meeting your firm has attended.

What happens if we cancel?

Nothing stops. fein runs on your servers. The graph, the connectors and every answer stay where they are. They keep working.
