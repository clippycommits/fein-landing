What teams ask about the integrations.

How is this different from Affinity or Attio?

A CRM is a database that your team fills in by hand. fein reads your CRM and everything around it, then resolves the whole picture into one graph that stays current on its own. It sits on top of the CRM and asks for no new data entry.

Do we have to replace anything?

No. fein replaces no system and migrates no data. It reads the record your team already makes, so if the CRM is out of date, fein is still current.

What does it connect to?

Gmail, Google Calendar, Drive, LinkedIn, Attio, Affinity, and Granola, with the rest of the wall above behind them. 30+ tools in all, and we add new connectors often.

Which AI tools can ask it questions?

Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, and Cursor, over one MCP endpoint. Any MCP client works, and each answer arrives with its source document.
