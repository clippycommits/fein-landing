What teams ask before the first call.

Why would we pay for software that is free?

You pay for 14 days instead of three months, and for keeping your own engineer off connector plumbing.

fein reads about thirty systems, each with its own authentication, rate limits, and idea of what a person is. Getting that to run against real data is hard, boring work. We have done it before.

Does our data ever reach you?

No. fein runs inside your environment, and the engineer works there with credentials you issue and can revoke. There is no telemetry, no analytics, and no control plane of ours. Disconnect the firm from the internet and fein carries on answering.

What do you actually need from us?

Somewhere to run it, admin consent on each source you want connected, and about 90 minutes of a partner’s time across the two calls.

Can we start self-hosted and move to managed later?

Yes, and a fair number of firms do. Call us when the maintenance stops being a sensible use of your engineer. We pick up what you have built rather than starting again, and the build fee is the same.

What if 14 days slips?

Then it is our problem, not your invoice. The build fee is fixed and the 30-day refund covers the whole engagement.
