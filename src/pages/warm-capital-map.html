What partners ask on the first call.

Who is this for?

A venture capital or private equity firm with a fund opening in the next eighteen months, or one managing re-ups on the fund it has. It works the same for both. Private equity firms tend to get more out of it, because they carry consultants and gatekeepers alongside direct LP relationships and those sit entirely in mail.

Does our data leave the firm?

No. fein is self-hosted and runs in your own environment, with your credentials. There is no telemetry and no control plane of ours. Every line of code that touches your data is open source and auditable before you run it.

What do we have to do?

Two short calls and the admin credentials for the sources you want read. We do everything in between. Nobody on your team enters data at any point, during the build or after it.

Does this replace our CRM?

No, and we would talk you out of it. DealCloud, Affinity, Salesforce and Attio stay exactly where they are. fein reads the CRM as one source among many and resolves the whole picture, which is why it stays current even when the CRM does not.

Are you a placement agent?

No. We are not compensated on capital raised, we take no percentage of a close, and we never contact an allocator on your behalf. You pay a flat build fee and a flat monthly fee for software and the engineer who runs it.

What happens when the raise closes?

The map keeps running, because the work does not stop at the close. It carries the re-up cycle for the fund you just raised: every promise made during the raise, tracked until it is met, and every relationship watched for the moment it goes quiet. Firms who stop paying keep the software and the map, on their own servers, under a licence we cannot revoke.

How fast can it start?

The 48 hour map starts as soon as we have read access. The full build is 14 days from the first call, and the date is a commitment rather than an estimate.
