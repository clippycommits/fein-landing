Questions

What deal teams ask first.

We already have DealCloud. Why do we need this?

A CRM is a database your team fills in by hand. It is only as current as the last person who updated it. fein is not a CRM, and it replaces nothing. It reads your CRM and everything around it. The whole picture resolves into one context graph. That graph stays current, and nobody touches a field.

fein also answers questions a CRM cannot. Relationship strength comes from real behaviour. Intermediaries get flagged when they go quiet. The reason you passed on a deal three years ago comes back with the document that says so.

Where does our data go?

Your data goes nowhere. fein runs on your infrastructure and sends nothing to us. There is no telemetry, and no control plane of ours in the path. Every line of code that touches your data is public. You can read it before you run anything.

What access does your engineer have?

Your IT grants access for the two calls and the work between them. That work runs on your infrastructure and under your logging. fein writes down every ingest, every merge and every decision with the person or agent that made it. Nothing is granted permanently. Access ends when the build ends, unless you ask us to keep the connectors supported.

What must we do?

You join two short calls. On the first call we connect your email, calendar, Drive, LinkedIn and CRM. We do all of the work between the calls. On the second call, fourteen days later, you check the answers against what you know. fein then goes live.

How far back does it read?

fein reads as far back as your mail retention allows. Most firms give us years of history on the first pass. That is the point. On day fourteen fein answers like a colleague who has been at your firm the whole time.

Can we run it ourselves?

Yes. fein is MIT licensed and the repository is public. The code we deploy for clients is the code you clone. The fee pays for the build and the upkeep. Your engineers then build on top of the graph instead of maintaining connectors.

What happens if we cancel?

Nothing stops. fein runs on your servers. The graph, the connectors and every answer stay where they are. They keep working.
