Questions

What deal teams ask first.

We already have DealCloud. Why do we need this?

A CRM is a database your team fills in by hand, and it is only ever as current as the last person who updated it. fein is not a CRM and it replaces nothing — it reads your CRM and everything around it, resolves the whole picture into one graph, and keeps that graph current without anyone touching a field.

What it adds is the part a CRM cannot do: relationship strength measured from real behaviour, intermediaries flagged when they go quiet, and the reason you passed on a deal three years ago with the document that says so.

Where does our data go?

Nowhere. fein runs on your infrastructure and sends nothing to us: no telemetry, and no control plane of ours anywhere in the path. Every line of code that touches your data is public, so you can read it before you run anything.

What access does your engineer have?

During the build, whatever your IT grants for the two calls and the work between them, on your infrastructure and under your logging — every ingest, merge and decision is written down with the person or agent that made it. Nothing is granted permanently, and access ends when the build does unless you ask us to keep supporting connectors.

What must we do?

Join two short calls. On the first we connect your email, calendar, Drive, LinkedIn and CRM. We do everything in between. On the second, fourteen days later, you check the answers against what you know, and then it goes live.

How far back does it read?

As far back as your mail retention allows, and most firms hand over years of history on the first pass. That is rather the point: on day fourteen fein answers like a colleague who has been at the firm the whole time.

Can we run it ourselves?

Yes. fein is MIT licensed and the repository is public, and the code we deploy for clients is the code you would clone. The fee pays for the build and the upkeep, so your engineers build on top of the graph instead of maintaining connectors.

What happens if we cancel?

Nothing stops. fein runs on your servers, so the graph, the connectors and every answer stay exactly where they are and keep working.
