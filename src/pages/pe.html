The problem

Your edge is proprietary deal flow. Nobody can see it.

  • Your coverage is a guess. Your firm has thousands of intermediary relationships. The CRM shows one picture. The inbox shows a different one. Nobody knows which picture is correct.
  • Relationships go quiet in silence. An intermediary sends you three deals. Then eleven months pass with no contact. You learn this when the next deal goes to a competitor.
  • Ownership leaves when people leave. A partner holds a relationship for six years. The partner moves to another firm. What the partner knew was never written down.
  • Deal history is not searchable. A company you passed on in 2023 returns at a lower multiple. The memo, the reason, and the intermediary sit in three systems and one person's memory.

How it works

Nobody enters data. The graph builds itself.

01

fein reads what your team already writes

It reads your email, calendar, Drive, LinkedIn, and CRM. It reads them continuously. It reads years of history on the first pass. Your deal team logs nothing and changes nothing.

02

fein resolves the record into one identity per person

It joins every duplicate copy of a contact into one record. It scores each relationship from real signal, not from a language model guess. When a fact stops being true, fein retires it. A retired fact keeps the dates it was true between, so your history stays queryable.
