fein.vc01

the problem

Your team's memory is scattered across the tools it works in.

The real record of every relationship sits in inboxes, calendars, notes and the CRM. Each tool holds a piece, and no tool holds the picture.

The CRM shows one picture, the inbox another.

Coverage is a guess, because the system of record is the one nobody keeps up to date.

Relationships go quiet in silence.

Nobody notices the drift until the next deal, or the next raise, goes somewhere warmer.

When a partner leaves, the memory leaves.

What they knew about a founder, an LP or a pass was never written anywhere.

Deal history is not searchable.

The memo, the reason and the introduction sit in three systems and one person's memory.

fein02

what fein is

One context graph of every relationship your team has.

fein reads your team's email, calendar, notes and CRM. It resolves them into one live graph: who exists, who knows whom, and how well. Then it answers in Claude, ChatGPT and Cursor, and every answer carries its source document.

01

Reads

Your inbox, calendar, Drive, LinkedIn and CRM, read continuously. Your team logs nothing.

02

Resolves

Every duplicate contact becomes one identity, scored from real signals, not an LLM guess.

03

Answers

One MCP endpoint. The same cited answer in every AI tool your team already uses.

fein03

what you can ask

Four questions your team asks every week.

  • Who is the warmest path to this founder?The weighted shortest path through your real graph, and the best colleague to make the introduction.
  • What should I know before this meeting?The attendees, how you know each of them, and what was open, on your screen before the meeting starts.
  • Why did we pass on this company?The meeting, the memo and the exact stated reason, each line traced to its source document.
  • Which relationships are going cold?The ones drifting from their own learned rhythm, flagged from dates and intervals, not a guess.

why it holds up

A graph your AI tools can trust, not a search box.

  • DeterministicThe same question gets the same answer every time, from structured graph traversal, not document scraping.
  • CitedThe document behind each line is one click away. Nothing has to be taken on faith.
  • Aware of timeA fact that stops being true is retired with its dates, not overwritten. You can ask what the team believed when it decided.
  • YoursIf you cancel, the software, the graph and every answer stay running on your servers.
